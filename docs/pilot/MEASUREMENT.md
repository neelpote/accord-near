# Pilot measurement and price test

Recruitment, retained use and paid continuation are **pending real participants**. No synthetic demo is counted as a customer. The signed-in wallet can inspect its setup/review counts under Pilot feedback; marked demo workspaces are excluded. Distinct wallets are not distinct teams and review days are not a retention claim. There is no automatic revenue claim.

Record with consent: previous workaround, time to detect/diagnose a real issue, actionable versus ignored alerts, repeated use, and response to explicit price/currency/period terms. Establish a before/after comparison on the same task; do not claim prevented losses or count every detection as useful.

Current unbilled pilot: code and key change observations, incidents and evidence exports. Existing paid offers remain independent finite agreements; the pilot has no automatic top-up or bundled charge. Optional explanations use the operator's capped Groq requests. Their cost is not inferred from request count and no API-credit purchase is performed.

Commercial hypothesis to test, not an implemented price: one application bundle with a disclosed observation frequency and an included investigation allowance. Ask participants whether a monthly package or prepaid finite check package is preferable, then offer a concrete continuation proposal they can decline. Do not hardcode a production subscription before feedback.

At the demonstration service price of 0.001 NEAR/report, sustained one-minute frequency means 1.44 NEAR/day and 43.2 NEAR/30 days per service, excluding gas/storage and requiring renewals of finite agreements. Five services would mean 216 NEAR/30 days. Do not sell this frequency by default: unchanged observations should be efficient, and AI should be requested on change or investigation. Any bundled financial model needs separate contract design/review; this release does not pretend to have it.

Progress gate: three real teams use a pilot and at least one accepts explicit continuation terms after use. Acceptance is not a payment. If users prefer their existing scripts and do not return, stop expanding the marketplace and revisit the value proposition.
