# Design-partner pilot

Status: recruitment pending. Five target conversations; zero conducted by this implementation.

Target a lead developer maintaining a deployed NEAR application, an engineer maintaining a relayer/bot, or an agency maintaining client applications. The buyer is an engineering lead, founder or agency owner. Avoid broad recruitment of token holders who do not operate software.

Ask about the last actual incident before showing Accord:

1. What changed, when, and how did you discover it?
2. How did you diagnose it? Ask for the existing workaround, alert or script.
3. How much engineering time did detection/diagnosis take?
4. Which notifications would you act on, and which would you mute?
5. What do existing tools cost in money or maintenance?
6. Would you configure a testnet application for a pilot? What prevents that?
7. After using it, would you accept a specific price and continuation period? Record the response verbatim with consent; do not interpret compliments as payment intent.

Store only consented, minimized notes. Do not collect credentials, private customer logs, emails or seed phrases. Wallet-authenticated pilot feedback in the app is a self-report, not proof that a company is a customer. Use pseudonymous references in private research notes if needed. No outreach has been sent.

Discovery gate: at least two distinct real teams connect an application and describe a concrete monitoring need. Owner must verify distinct teams manually; distinct wallets are not sufficient. Paid-continuation gate: a real participant accepts explicit price/currency/period terms after pilot use; acceptance is not a payment or revenue.

If scripts already suffice, stop expanding the dashboard and investigate whether bounded-payment infrastructure is the stronger need. Do not pivot to an SDK without interviewing that different customer segment.
